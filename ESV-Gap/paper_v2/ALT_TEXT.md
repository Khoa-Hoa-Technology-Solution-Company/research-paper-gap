# Alternative text for figures (AMI 2027 paper #162)

**Fig. 1.** Flowchart of the ESV-Gap control loop. The top row runs left to right: Observe (screen and ingest), Hypothesize (graph signals), Local gates (ten non-compensatory checks) and Verify (four pillars). Verify branches into three blocks: Pillar A, two-stage external probing, where Stage A1 lexical candidate retrieval feeds Stage A2 semantic evidence extraction; Pillar B, disjoint corroboration; and Pillars C and D, extractor and saturation diagnostics. All three feed a box labelled Reason and Decide (deterministic policy pi). Its output is an epistemic disposition (refuted, supported or review), which the policy maps to an executed action (reject, retain or abstain).

**Fig. 2.** Line and bar chart over the number of papers ingested, from 1 to 150. A solid black line shows cumulative canonical entities, rising almost linearly from near 0 to 1,185. A dotted line, the Heaps' law fit V(n) = 8.03 n^0.995, lies on top of it. Grey bars on the right axis show new entities contributed by each paper, between 0 and 17. A dashed 7-paper moving average fluctuates between about 4 and 11 without a downward trend. Together they show that the vocabulary is not saturating.
